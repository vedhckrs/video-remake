## 2. Upload flow (Instagram app)

1. Copy the MP4 and the cover JPG to your phone (AirDrop, Google Drive or USB). Don't send them through WhatsApp, because it recompresses the video.
2. Instagram → **+** → **Reel** → pick `SSD-Explainer_1080x1920_60fps.mp4` → **Next**.
3. **Don't add music or filters.** The Reel already has its own mixed voice, music and sound effects. Keep the original audio at 100%.
4. Paste the **Caption** (section 3).
5. Tap **Edit cover** → **Add from camera roll** → choose `reel_cover_1080x1920.jpg`.
   - In the **Profile grid** crop, drag the image down slightly so the whole headline "INSIDE THE CHIP" shows with space above it. Match `grid_preview_1080x1440.jpg`.
   - Tap **Done**. **The cover can't be changed after posting**, so check it now.
6. **Rename audio** (if offered): `How an SSD stores your files`
7. **Add topics** (if offered): Technology · Science · Computers
8. **Advanced settings**:
   - **Add AI label: ON.** The narration is an AI voice and the cover image was made with AI. Meta asks for the label on realistic AI audio or images.
   - **Captions / auto-generated captions: OFF** (if the toggle is there). The captions are already burned in, and auto-captions would show twice.
   - Hide like counts: your choice.
   - **Allow people to download your Reel**: your choice (On helps sharing).
9. **Share to Facebook**: optional. Turn it on if you also post there.
10. **Trial reel** (if offered): leave it off for this one so your followers see it.
11. **Share**. Wait about 15 to 30 minutes for full HD processing before you judge quality on the phone.
