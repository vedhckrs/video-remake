**Rules used**

- `img/` = cleared for on-screen: Wikimedia Commons files under CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA (author, licence and file page recorded), and official Apple Newsroom press images (marked "press image, editorial use; check terms").
- `ref/` = reference only, never on screen: iFixit (CC BY-NC-SA, non-commercial) and TechInsights (all rights reserved).
- CC BY-SA files: showing them needs the credit line; if a frame is a modified version of the photo, the share-alike term applies to that adapted image. Owner to decide how to handle this (see the last section).
- Wikimedia rate-limits full-size originals for automated clients and asks for its standard thumbnail sizes instead, so most Commons files here are Wikimedia's own 3840 / 1920 / 1280 / 960 / 500 px renditions. The full-size original is one click away on each file page.
- Anything marked *uncertain* was not confirmed by a second source. Marking text is quoted as legible in the photo; re-check against the image before putting text on screen.

### Drawing notes - iPhone 18 Pro (2026) exterior

- Size: 150.0 x 71.9 x 8.75 mm, 211 g, 6.3-inch display, 2622 x 1206 px (iFixit device page). Aspect of the body is about 2.09 : 1.
- Colours (Apple): black, silver, glacier (pale blue), burgundy (new). Lens rings and frame match the body colour; the inset back glass panel is slightly lighter/more matte than the frame in the renders.
- Back: one raised camera plateau running the full width at the top (roughly the top 30% of the back, measured by eye). Three large lenses at the left in a triangle - two stacked at the far left, one to their right at mid-height. At the far right, top to bottom: flash, microphone pinhole, LiDAR dot. Below the plateau: rounded-rectangle glass panel with a centred glossy Apple logo.
- Front: flat glass, thin even black bezel, small pill-shaped Dynamic Island; the Face ID infrared camera now sits under the display to the upper left of the island (Apple, iFixit, Commons uploader agree).
- Edges: Action button and volume buttons on the left, power button and Camera Control on the right (iFixit). Exact button lengths: take from the Apple colour-lineup render.
- Body material: aluminium unibody (iFixit; Apple mentions recycled aluminium in the enclosure).

### Drawing notes - iPhone 18 Pro internals / teardown

- Layout seen from the screen side: cameras across the top, logic board in the upper part beside/below the camera block, battery filling the middle and lower body, speaker bottom left and Taptic Engine bottom right, USB-C port bottom centre with a long flex up one side.
- Logic board: two-layer sandwich, L / T outline (wide rectangle with a narrow arm), black solder mask with copper-orange edges and silk-screen. Board numbers seen: 820-04304-13 (SoC side), 820-04305-09 (RF half).
- A20 Pro is on the OUTSIDE of the sandwich under a square copper/gold-toned lid with an outlined Apple logo, pressed against the vapor chamber through a grey mesh-like thermal pad. NAND is inside the sandwich (iFixit).
- Battery: L-shaped steel can in a screwed-in metal tray (13 screws per iFixit). eSIM model label: 4288 mAh / 16.758 Wh. iFixit's spec list gives 4,056 mAh - probably the physical-SIM model (uncertain).
- Vapor chamber / tray: stamped steel plate with a regular dimple grid; Apple says 3x the surface area of iPhone 17 Pro.
- Rear cameras: three modules in one metal bracket; main camera has a 6-blade variable aperture (f/1.48 to f/4.0). Front: selfie camera and dot projector in the island, IR camera under the display.
- Taptic Engine: black rectangular bar labelled 'TAPTIC ENGINE' at the bottom right. Exact dimensions not given by any source.

### Drawing notes - Apple A20 Pro (package and die)

- Package as seen in the phone: a nearly square metal lid, copper/gold-toned in iFixit's photo and silver in TechInsights' (lighting or unit difference - uncertain), outlined Apple logo in the centre, one line of small text along an edge ('BL21HF72059' on iFixit's unit). No 'A20' text is visible on the lid in either photo; an 'APL' part number for A20 Pro was not found in any free source.
- Inside the package (Apple, TechInsights): processor die and LPDDR5X memory sit SIDE BY SIDE in a wafer-level multi-chip module, not stacked. Draw the die and memory next to each other.
- Die: about 8.0 x 12.3 mm, about 98 mm2 (TechInsights estimate). Process: TSMC N2, gate-all-around nanosheets.
- Apple's numbers: 6-core CPU, 7-core GPU, Dual 16-core Neural Engine, 50% more memory bandwidth than A19 Pro.
- Previous generation for contrast: A19 Pro is a black moulded package marked 'APL1V12 339S01837' with DRAM stacked on top (iFixit).
- Floorplan (where CPU / GPU / NPU blocks sit): unknown from reliable sources - do not draw a 'real' floorplan.

### Drawing notes - OLED sub-pixel macro (diamond layout)

- Pattern (all three real macros agree): red and blue sub-pixels alternate on a square grid turned 45 degrees; green sub-pixels are smaller and sit between them, so there are two greens for every red and every blue.
- Shapes: blue is the largest (rounded diamond), red slightly smaller (rounded diamond), green smallest (round to oval; on the iPhone 18 Pro panel the green ovals tilt alternately left and right).
- Background between emitters is black; at 40x fine gold-coloured wiring is visible between sub-pixels (iPhone 18 Pro, iFixit).
- iPhone 18 Pro pixel density is 460 ppi (iFixit spec list), so one pixel pitch is about 55 micrometres (calculated).
- Over the under-display Face ID camera every other row of pixels is removed (iFixit) - optional detail.

- Structure: source and drain blocks at each end, joined by a vertical stack of thin horizontal silicon sheets (the diagrams here and IBM's 2021 device use three); the gate wraps every sheet on all four sides.
- TSMC N2 is the first TSMC node with this structure; power delivery stays on the front side; TSMC's version includes inner spacers (TechInsights).
- Sheet dimensions for TSMC N2: not published in any free source found. IBM's 2021 research device: 12 nm gate length, 44 nm pitch (press coverage; sheet width is reported differently by different outlets - uncertain).
- '2 nm' is a node name, not a measured feature size - avoid drawing a literal 2 nm ruler.
