# 6 AI agents that work for you 24/7: Instagram Reels upload guide

Package made 25 Nov 2026. Everything here is ready to paste.

## Quick start (about 5 minutes)

1. Instagram → **+** → **Reel** → pick `Six-AI-Agents_1080x1920_60fps.mp4` (main folder). Add no music and no filters.
2. Paste the caption (section 3), set the cover to `Six-AI-Agents_Thumbnail_1080x1920.jpg` (main folder) and fix the grid crop.
3. Turn the AI label **ON** and auto-captions **OFF**, then share and pin the comment.

The full steps are below.

## 2. Upload flow (Instagram app)

1. Copy the MP4 and the cover JPG to your phone (AirDrop, Google Drive or USB). Don't send them through WhatsApp, because it recompresses the video. If you must use WhatsApp, send them as a **Document**, not as a video or photo.
2. Instagram → **+** → **Reel** → pick `Six-AI-Agents_1080x1920_60fps.mp4` → **Next**.
3. **Don't add music or filters.** The Reel already has its own mixed voice, music and sound effects. Keep the original audio at 100%.
4. Paste the **Caption** (section 3).
5. Tap **Edit cover** → **Add from camera roll** → choose `Six-AI-Agents_Thumbnail_1080x1920.jpg`.
   - In the **Profile grid** crop, drag the image **down** (toward the top of the picture) so her whole face, the "FREE vs $100" line and the phone all show. Match `grid_preview_1080x1440.jpg`.
   - Tap **Done**. **The cover can't be changed after posting**, so check it now.
6. **Rename audio** (if offered): `6 AI agents compared`
7. **Add topics** (if offered): Technology · Artificial intelligence · Productivity
8. **Advanced settings**:
   - **Add AI label: ON.** The narration is an AI voice and the cover photo was made with AI. Meta asks for the label on realistic AI audio or images.
   - **Captions / auto-generated captions: OFF** (if the toggle is there). The captions are already burned in, and auto-captions would show twice.
   - Hide like counts: your choice.
   - **Allow people to download your Reel**: your choice (On helps sharing).
9. **Share to Facebook**: optional. Turn it on if you also post there.
10. **Trial reel** (if offered): leave it off for this one so your followers see it.
11. **Share**. Wait about 15 to 30 minutes for full HD processing before you judge quality on the phone.
