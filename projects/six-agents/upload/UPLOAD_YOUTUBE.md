## 2. Upload flow (phone first, then desktop Studio)

YouTube Studio on desktop can't change a Short's thumbnail, so set the thumbnail on the phone and everything else on desktop.

1. **Phone, YouTube app**: **+** → **Upload a video** → `Six-AI-Agents_1080x1920_60fps.mp4` → **Next**. Copy the file to the phone first; don't use a WhatsApp copy. Don't add music: the video already has its own mixed music and sound effects.
2. On the details screen, tap the ✏️ pencil on the thumbnail:
   - If you see an option to **add/upload an image**, choose `Six-AI-Agents_Thumbnail_1080x1920.jpg`.
   - If you can only pick a frame, scrub to **0:06**, the price ladder with all six agents (see `frame_pick_0m06s.jpg`).
3. Paste the **Title**, set **Visibility: Private** for now, and upload.
4. **Desktop, studio.youtube.com** → Content → open the Short → **Details**:
   - paste the **Description** and **Tags** (Show more → Tags);
   - set every setting in section 4;
   - click **Save**.
5. **Subtitles** → Add language → **English** → **Upload file** → **With timing** → `captions_en.srt` → Publish.
6. Check the Short once on your phone (sound, first frame, captions toggle), then set **Visibility: Public**. Or use **Schedule** for the time your viewers are most active (Studio → Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube").
7. Post the **pinned comment** (section 5) and pin it.

## 6. Thumbnail: how it was made

Made on Magnific with **GPT Image 2.6 · Sunburst** (photoreal viral style, same recipe as the fps video's approved cover): 9:16, 2K, Quality High, Thinking High, 1 image, **175 credits**. The whole image is generated; the woman is not a real individual, and the six fluffy helpers on her phone are generic characters (not the companies' mascots).
- She faces the camera and **shows** the phone (screen toward the viewer) at chest height, pointing at it with an excited face.
- The headline "FREE vs $100" sits just above the phone: green "FREE", small white "vs", red "$100". It matches the video: OpenClaw and Hermes are free; Dots starts with ChatGPT Pro at $100/month. No logos and no brand names.
- `Six-AI-Agents_Thumbnail_master_1520x2688.png` (main folder) is the original; the 1080 × 1920 JPG is a resized copy. The image was downloaded and then moved to the Magnific trash.
