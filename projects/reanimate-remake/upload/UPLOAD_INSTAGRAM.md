## 2. Set up the "Comment ANIMATE" DM before posting

The voiceover ends with "Comment ANIMATE and I'll send you the link", so every ANIMATE comment needs a DM. Pick one option:

**Option A: automatic, recommended.** Use ManyChat (free plan) or any Meta-approved comment-to-DM tool with an Instagram professional (Creator or Business) account.
1. Connect your Instagram account in ManyChat.
2. Create an automation: trigger **"User comments on your post or reel"**, choose this Reel (or "any post"), keyword **ANIMATE**. Matching usually ignores case, so "animate" also works. Check the setting.
3. Turn on the **public reply to the comment**:
   ```
   Sent! Check your DMs 📩
   ```
4. Set the **DM message**:
   ```
   Here you go 👇
   github.com/NiazMorshed2007/reanimate

   reanimate is free and open source (MIT). Point it at any site, screen recording, GIF or Lottie file, and it rebuilds the animation in CSS, Framer Motion or GSAP. Install steps are in the README. Have fun cloning 🎬
   ```
5. Test it from a second account before you post the Reel publicly.

**Option B: manual.** Reply to each ANIMATE comment with "Sent! Check your DMs 📩" and paste the DM text above into their DMs. Check comments often in the first 24 hours.

(Optional: put the link in your bio as well, for people who don't comment.)

## 3. Upload flow (Instagram app)

1. Copy the MP4 and cover JPG to your phone (AirDrop, Google Drive or USB, not WhatsApp).
2. Instagram → **+** → **Reel** → pick `Reanimate_1080x1920_60fps.mp4` → **Next**.
3. **Don't add music or filters.** The original audio already has the voice, music and sound effects mixed. Keep it at 100%.
4. Paste the **Caption** (section 4).
5. **Edit cover** → **Add from camera roll** → `reel_cover_1080x1920.jpg`.
   - In the **Profile grid** crop, drag the image down slightly so "FRAME-PERFECT COPY" shows in full with space above it. Match `grid_preview_1080x1440.jpg`.
   - Tap **Done**. **The cover can't be changed after posting.**
6. **Rename audio** (if offered): `Copy any web animation`
7. **Add topics** (if offered): Technology · Web Development · Design
8. **Advanced settings**:
   - **Add AI label: ON.** The narration is an AI voice and the cover is AI-generated.
   - **Auto-generated captions: OFF** (if the toggle is there), because the captions are burned in.
   - Allow downloads: your choice.
9. **Share to Facebook**: optional. **Trial reel**: off.
10. **Share**, then wait about 15 to 30 minutes for HD processing.
